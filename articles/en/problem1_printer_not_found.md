## Problem Solving

1. Wifi devices can connect to Ethernet connected devices
2. Wifi can not acces other wifi connected devices
3. Ethernet connected devices can ping wlan connected devices
4. enabling and disabling allowed communication bete
5. Wifi ap only has connection in the higher floors of the house
6. higher floors can ping everything
7. floors below can not
8. restart of ap

After the restart of the AP, the Printer suddenly worked
