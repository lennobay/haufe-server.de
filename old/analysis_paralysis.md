Learning skills often sets one into to the trap of the Dunning Kruger Effect. This lets one believe, that their knowledge in that domain is complete, but it is not. But when you realise you where caught by the Dunning Kruger Effect, you start to question your own capabilities, which is good do not get me wrong but it can lead to obsesive overthinking.
## Others being better then you
So the thing with overthinking is that you are fine until you face the real world. Someone else is "better" then you and you start to question your life choises. One can image that with school. 
The student next to you, that learned less has a better outcome, you might not be angry at that person but you are unhappy. You think that you own intelligence is low and you are not capable to do anything. But an even worser thing it is when you, are worse then a person that has no background in the area or it is and area of pession like a hobby.
Such thing happend to me and I can say myself that it was not a good expierience. Now you can think oh everything ok, not a big deal but for the person it is in deed a big deal. Especially when it is a passion just like for me. 
I accompanied a workshop for 13 to 14 years old as a Jugendbegleiter (google it) and perfomed worse then the person that had a smaller background then me in that area. One thing that can help in the situtation is to think about 4 things. How did the other person teach , how could this teaching influence the student, do you realy take interest in that subarea and is it so world changing.
Number one, the how is the person teaching. I think that every person teaches diffrent and the real part of the students doing what they want is diffrent. First of all, I have the approach of asking questions and wanting the students to do it my self. But this leades and lead to little progess because I let the students do and when you have not preperation you just do not progress in youre head because it is not your coding style. On the opossit side the other person might be applying the same principle as you or just give the student less freedom. By providing him with information how to do it and let the student perfect it. This might be an exuse for your incompetence or just a fact. And with that question two is also already answered.
